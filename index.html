    THE PRECISE CLAIM

    Missing proof is
    not permission.

    In the tested demonstration, an unresolved execution start prevents a subsequent reservation through the same gate and intact ledger. The external effect is preserved; the uncertainty is preserved too.

    That is a bounded behaviour, not a claim of exactly-once execution or protection against every failure. This page illustrates the behaviour; it does not verify a running backend.

    Where the claim stops

    • Requires the same intact ledger and execution through the gate. Bypassing the gate is outside the claim.
    • Unresolved work can block other reservations on that ledger. Availability is a real trade-off.
    • Recorded terminal failures may allow future work; try that scenario above.
    • Hash links alone are not an independently authenticated anchor against a writer who can rewrite the chain.
    • The naive retry is an illustrative comparison, not a benchmark of other products.
    • No real messages, payments or client jobs are executed here.
